A little letter for you 💌

Happiest birthday to my fighter!!! 🦖🥳🎂❤️

I hope you have the kind of birthday that leaves you with a big smile on your face and a heart full of good memories. You deserve a day where you can relax, laugh, eat way too much good food, and just enjoy being you. 🤍

I kept thinking about what I wanted to write to you, and I realized I didn't want to make this just another “happy birthday” message. I wanted to remind you of something a little more meaningful.

When I think about us, it's not just the big moments that come to mind. It's all the little things.

The random conversations that somehow lasted forever. The stupid jokes that probably weren't even that funny. The moments where we could laugh over absolutely nothing. The little looks, the hugs, and all those tiny moments that probably didn't seem important at the time but somehow became some of my favorite memories.

And yes… I still remember the little butterflies you used to get when I called you my little princess. 🥹❤️

I don't think you realized how cute you were in those moments.

I'm really thankful that I got to know you the way I did. Not just the version of you everyone else sees, but the person underneath it all. Your funny side, your stubborn side, your soft side, and the side of you that keeps pushing forward even when things aren't easy.

You taught me that some people can become such an important part of your life without you even realizing how much they're going to mean to you.

And you became that person for me.

I still love and care about you very much. ❤️ I don't say that because I expect anything from you. I just think some feelings deserve to be honest, especially on a day that's about celebrating someone who means so much to you.

I'm proud of you for everything you've already overcome, and I hope you never forget how capable you are of becoming everything you want to be. Keep chasing the things that make you excited about your future. Keep growing. Keep learning. Keep being stubborn when it comes to the things you believe in. And most importantly, keep being yourself.

I pray that God and Jesus are always beside you, protecting you, guiding you, giving you strength, and watching over you wherever life takes you. 🙏🏻❤️ I pray that this next year brings you closer to your goals, gives you moments you'll never forget, and brings you the kind of happiness that stays.

Whatever this next chapter looks like, I genuinely hope it is a beautiful one for you.

So today, don't think too much. Don't stress about tomorrow. Just enjoy your day. Laugh a lot, eat lots, let people spoil you a little, and make some new memories!!❤️

And if you ever look back at this little birthday surprise, I hope it makes you smile not because of the words on this page, but because of all the little memories behind them.

Happy birthday, my fighter. 🦖❤️

I'm always going to be rooting for you.
Your little peaches 🐣❤️
